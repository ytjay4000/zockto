## What a Video Agency Actually Costs

We surveyed pricing from 15 video production agencies across India (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad) to get realistic numbers. Here's what **video agency costs** look like in 2026:

### Per-Video Costs

- **Basic UGC-style ad (15-30s):** ₹15,000 - ₹30,000
- **Mid-range product ad (30-60s):** ₹25,000 - ₹50,000
- **Premium brand video (60s+):** ₹50,000 - ₹3,00,000
- **UGC creator content (per creator):** ₹5,000 - ₹25,000

### What's Included (Usually)

- Script writing (1-2 revisions)
- Talent sourcing and booking
- Studio or location shoot
- Basic editing and post-production
- 1-2 rounds of revisions
- Final delivery in common formats

### Hidden Costs Most Agencies Don't Mention Upfront

- **Extra revisions:** ₹2,000-5,000 per round after the included ones
- **Multiple formats:** Need the same ad in 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9? That's extra.
- **Rush delivery:** 25-50% surcharge for "fast" turnaround
- **Language versions:** ₹5,000-10,000 per additional language
- **A/B variations:** Each variation is basically a new video (new invoice)

### Typical Timeline

- **Briefing and scripting:** 2-4 days
- **Talent booking:** 3-7 days
- **Filming:** 1-2 days
- **Editing:** 3-5 days
- **Revisions:** 2-5 days
- **Total:** 2-4 weeks (often longer)

So for a single UGC-style video ad, you're looking at **₹15,000-50,000 and 2-4 weeks**. Need 10 ads for A/B testing? Budget ₹1.5-5 lakh and 2-3 months.
