## The Old Way: Why Traditional Ad Production Doesn't Scale

Let's be honest about what traditional product ad production actually involves:

### For Product Photos
- Rent a studio or set up a makeshift one (₹2,000-₹10,000)
- Buy or rent lighting equipment (₹5,000-₹15,000)
- Hire a photographer (₹5,000-₹25,000 per session)
- Style and set up each product shot
- Post-processing and retouching (₹500-₹2,000 per image)
- Timeline: 2-5 days per product line

### For Video Ads
- Write a script (₹2,000-₹10,000 if outsourced)
- Hire a UGC creator or actor (₹12,000-₹40,000 per video)
- Film with proper equipment
- Edit, add music, captions, transitions (₹5,000-₹15,000)
- Revisions and final output
- Timeline: 5-14 days per video

Total cost for a single product: ₹30,000-₹1,00,000+ for a photo set and 2-3 video ads. For a brand with 20+ products, the math simply doesn't work — especially if you want to test multiple creative variations.

And here's the real killer: **most of those ads won't even be your best performers**. Performance marketing is a numbers game. You need many variations to find the winners, and traditional production makes that financially impossible for most businesses.

## Tips for Getting the Best Quality

AI tools are powerful, but garbage in = garbage out. Here's how to maximize the quality of your camera-free ads:

### Product Image Quality
- **Use the best image you have.** A smartphone photo in good lighting beats a blurry DSLR shot. Natural daylight near a window is your best friend.
- **Clean, simple background.** White or neutral backgrounds give AI tools the best starting point for background replacement.
- **Show the product clearly.** No clutter, no obstructions. The AI needs to understand what it's working with.
- **Multiple angles help.** If you can take 2-3 product photos from different angles, the AI has more to work with.

### Script and Messaging
- **Edit the auto-generated script.** AI scripts are good starting points but benefit from human refinement. Add your brand voice and specific product claims.
- **Lead with the hook.** The first sentence should stop scrollers. "I stopped spending ₹3,000 a month on [category] when I found this" beats "Hi, let me tell you about a great product."
- **Include specific numbers.** "Rated 4.8 stars by 5,000+ customers" is more compelling than "highly rated."

### Avatar Selection
- **Match your audience.** Your avatar should look like your target customer's peer, not your brand's CEO.
- **Test demographics.** Try different age groups, appearances, and presentation styles. The right avatar can improve conversion rates by 20-40%.
- **Consider voice tone.** Enthusiastic for impulse purchases, calm and authoritative for premium products.
