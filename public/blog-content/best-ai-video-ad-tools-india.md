## What Matters for Indian Businesses

Before we rank the tools, let's talk about what makes an **AI ad maker** actually useful in the Indian context:

- **INR pricing:** No forex markup, no surprise charges. Indian businesses shouldn't pay a "dollar tax" for AI tools.
- **Indian language support:** Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali — your customers don't all speak English.
- **Indian avatars and accents:** Authentic representation matters. A blonde American avatar won't connect with your Tier-2 audience.
- **UPI/Indian payment support:** Accepting Indian cards and UPI without friction.
- **Platform compatibility:** Tools should work well for Indian ad platforms — Meta, YouTube, Amazon India, Flipkart, Meesho.
- **Value for money:** Indian SMBs watch every rupee. Cost per video matters more than feature lists.

InVideo AI lets you describe what you want in text, and it generates a video with stock footage, text overlays, and voiceover.
