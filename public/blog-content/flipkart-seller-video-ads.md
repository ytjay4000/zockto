## Types of Flipkart Product Videos That Work

Not all product videos are created equal. Here's what actually moves the needle on Flipkart:

### 1. Product Showcase Videos (15-30 seconds)

These are your bread and butter. A quick, clean video showing the product from multiple angles with key features highlighted. Think of it as an animated version of your image gallery — but way more engaging.

### 2. UGC-Style Review Videos (30-60 seconds)

A person (or AI avatar) talking about the product like they're recommending it to a friend. This style builds massive trust because it feels authentic rather than corporate. For Flipkart buyers who rely heavily on reviews, this format is gold.

### 3. How-To / Demo Videos (30-60 seconds)

Show the product being used. If you sell a blender, show it blending. If you sell a face serum, show the application. Simple, but incredibly effective for reducing "will this work?" anxiety.

### 4. Comparison Videos (30-45 seconds)

Position your product against the "generic alternative" without naming competitors. Show why your product is the better choice. This works particularly well in categories like electronics and skincare.

## Flipkart Video Specs & Requirements

Before you create anything, here's what Flipkart expects:

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Format | MP4 |
| Resolution | Minimum 720p (1080p recommended) |
| Aspect Ratio | 16:9 or 1:1 |
| Duration | 15-60 seconds |
| File Size | Under 50MB |
| Audio | Optional but recommended |
| Content Rules | No competitor mentions, no misleading claims |

The sweet spot? A **1080p, 30-second video** with clean audio and text overlays. This hits the perfect balance between information density and viewer retention.

## Optimizing Your Flipkart Video Ads for Conversions

Creating the video is step one. Optimizing it for maximum conversions is where the real wins happen.

### Hook in the First 3 Seconds

Flipkart shoppers are scroll-happy. Your video needs to grab attention immediately. Start with the product's biggest benefit or a bold claim — not a logo animation.

### Show the Product Within 5 Seconds

Don't make people wait. Show the actual product early. Flipkart buyers want to see what they're getting, not watch an intro sequence.

### Use Text Overlays

Many Flipkart users browse with sound off (commuting, at work, etc.). Text overlays ensure your key selling points come through even on mute. Include price, key features, and any offers.

### End with a Clear CTA

Sounds basic, but so many sellers forget this. End your video with "Add to Cart Now" or "Order Today — Limited Stock." A direct CTA can improve click-through by 15-20%.

### Match Your Listing Copy

Your video and listing text should tell the same story. If your video highlights "waterproof," make sure your bullet points and description also emphasize waterproofing. Consistency builds trust.

## Common Mistakes Flipkart Sellers Make with Video

After seeing hundreds of Flipkart seller videos, here are the most common pitfalls:

1. **Making it too long.** Nobody wants to watch a 3-minute product video on a ₹499 item. Keep it tight — 15-30 seconds for simple products, 60 seconds max for complex ones.
2. **Poor audio quality.** Buzzy microphone audio or no audio at all. Either get clean voiceover or go silent with text overlays. AI-generated voiceovers solve this completely.
3. **Generic stock footage.** Buyers can smell stock footage from a mile away. Show your actual product. AI tools like Zockto solve this by generating video from your real product images.
4. **Ignoring mobile optimization.** If your text is too small to read on a phone screen, it might as well not exist. Design for 6-inch screens first.
5. **Not adding video to ALL listings.** Most sellers add video to their top 2-3 products and ignore the rest. With AI generation, there's no excuse not to cover your entire catalog.
