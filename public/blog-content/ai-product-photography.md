## The Product Photography Bottleneck

Product photography is one of the most underestimated costs in e-commerce. Let's break down what it actually takes:

### The Traditional Approach
- **Photographer:** ₹2,000-₹10,000 per hour
- **Studio rental:** ₹3,000-₹15,000 per session
- **Props and staging:** ₹1,000-₹5,000 per setup
- **Retouching:** ₹200-₹1,000 per image
- **Total cost per product:** ₹2,000-₹10,000 (for 3-5 images)
- **Timeline:** 3-7 days for a batch of products

For a catalog of 100 products, you're looking at ₹2-10 lakhs just for photography. For a new business launching with limited capital? That's often budget that has to come from somewhere else — usually ad spend, which means fewer eyeballs on those beautiful photos you just paid for.

It's a frustrating Catch-22: you need great photos to sell, but you need sales to afford great photos.

### The DIY Approach
Many sellers try to DIY with a smartphone, a white sheet, and some natural light. And honestly? It shows. Inconsistent lighting, shaky framing, backgrounds that aren't quite white — these small quality issues add up to lower consumer trust and reduced conversions.

The technical process behind AI product photography is fascinating, but for practical purposes, here's the workflow:

1. **Upload your product image.** This can be a smartphone photo, an existing product image, or even a screenshot from a supplier's catalog. The AI is forgiving about input quality — it just needs to clearly see your product.
2. **AI isolates the product.** Advanced background removal separates your product from whatever background it's currently on.

4. **AI generates the image.** The AI places your product in the chosen scene, adjusts lighting and shadows to match, adds realistic reflections, and renders a high-resolution output.

## Getting the Best Quality From AI

AI product photography quality depends significantly on your input. Here's how to get the best results:

### Input Image Tips
- **Use good lighting.** Natural daylight or a well-lit area. Shadows on the product create shadows in the AI output.
- **Shoot against a simple background.** White or plain solid color. This helps the AI isolate the product cleanly.
- **Get close.** Fill the frame with your product. More product detail = better AI output.
- **Keep it sharp.** Blurry input = blurry output. Tap to focus on your phone camera.
- **Remove packaging if possible.** Show the actual product, not the box (unless the box IS the product).

### Generation Tips
- **Generate multiple variations.** AI output has some randomness. Generate 3-5 versions and pick the best.
- **Mix styles.** Don't just do white backgrounds. Include lifestyle images — they perform better on social media and can increase product page conversion by 20-30%.
- **Match your platform.** Amazon wants white backgrounds for main images. Instagram wants lifestyle. Shopify benefits from both. Generate different styles for different placements.
