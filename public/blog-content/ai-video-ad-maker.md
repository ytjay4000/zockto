## Why E-Commerce Brands Need Video Ads (Yesterday)

Before we dive into tools, let's talk about *why* this matters. If you're still relying on static product images for your ads, here's the reality check:

- **Social platforms prioritize video.** Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook all algorithmically boost video content. Your reach is literally higher with video.
- **Attention spans favor motion.** The average user scrolls 90 meters of content per day on their phone. Video stops thumbs; static images don't.

The question isn't whether you need video ads. It's how to create them efficiently without blowing your budget.

## Getting Started: Your First AI Video Ad

Ready to try it? Here's a practical step-by-step to create your first AI video ad:

1. **Sign up for a free account.** Most tools (including Zockto) offer free credits to get started.
2. **Start with your best-selling product.** Don't experiment with new products. Use something you already know converts — you want to compare AI video performance against your existing ad performance.
3. **Create 3-5 variations.** Same product, different hooks and avatars. This gives you data on what works.
4. **Run them as a split test.** Use your ad platform's A/B testing feature. Set equal budgets. Let them run for 3-5 days.
5. **Analyze and iterate.** Which hook performed best? Which avatar? Now create 5 more variations based on the winner.

> "The brands that win at performance marketing aren't the ones with the best single ad. They're the ones who test the most variations and find winners fastest." — This is exactly what AI video ad makers enable.
