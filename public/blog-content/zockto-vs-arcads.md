## Feature-by-Feature Comparison

### Video Length
This is one of the biggest differentiators. **Zockto supports 60-second videos; Arcads caps at approximately 30 seconds.**

Why does this matter? Because not every product can be sold in 15-30 seconds. Supplements, skincare routines, tech gadgets, premium products — these often need 45-60 seconds to properly communicate value. The problem-solution-proof-CTA framework that drives D2C conversions works best with at least 30-40 seconds, and having 60 seconds gives you room for proper storytelling.

If you only run short-form ads on TikTok and Instagram Reels, the 30-second cap might be fine. But if you advertise on YouTube, Facebook Feed, or Google, longer videos consistently outperform shorter ones for conversion.

### Avatar Library
Zockto offers 150+ avatars to Arcads' 100+. Both offer avatar cloning — you can create a custom avatar from your own likeness or a brand ambassador's likeness.

Arcads' avatars are known for their premium quality. The expressions and micro-movements are very natural. Zockto's avatars have improved significantly and are convincing at scroll-speed viewing, which is what matters for ad performance.

Honest take: for the purpose of running ads (not presentations or training videos), both libraries are more than sufficient. The difference in avatar quality matters less than the difference in your hook and script.

### Voice and Language
Zockto leads here with 200+ voices across 48 languages, compared to Arcads' 30+ languages. Both offer voice cloning.

For brands selling internationally or in multilingual markets like India, the broader language support is a tangible advantage. You can create the same ad in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, and English in one sitting — which is powerful for regional D2C targeting.

### Templates
Zockto offers 100+ templates specifically designed for e-commerce ads. Arcads offers 50+ templates. Both are regularly updated.

Templates matter because they determine the visual structure of your ad — text placement, transitions, product showcasing style. More templates = more variety in your creative testing, which is important for avoiding ad fatigue.

### AI Product Photography
This is a Zockto-only feature. Product Ad Shot lets you generate professional product photography using AI — place your product in studio-quality lifestyle scenes without an actual photoshoot.

If you're an e-commerce brand that also needs product images (for your website, social posts, or image ads), this is a genuine bonus. Arcads doesn't offer this.

### Workflow and UX
Zockto's 5-click workflow is deliberately minimal: upload product → pick avatar → choose template → generate → download. There's very little to learn and almost nothing to configure.

Arcads offers more customization options — you have more control over scripting, scene structure, and output. If you want granular creative control, Arcads gives you more levers to pull.

The trade-off is speed. Zockto optimizes for getting a finished video as fast as possible. Arcads optimizes for giving you more control over the result. Neither approach is "wrong" — it depends on your workflow preference.
