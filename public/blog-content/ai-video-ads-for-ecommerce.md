## The Old Way of Making Product Videos is Broken

Traditional product video production looks something like this:

1. Hire a videographer ($500-$2,000 per shoot day)
2. Find and hire talent/models ($200-$1,000 per person)
3. Rent a studio or find a location ($200-$500)
4. Ship products for the shoot
5. Wait for editing (1-2 weeks)
6. Request revisions (another week)
7. Get final files

**Total cost: $1,500-$5,000+ per video. Total time: 2-4 weeks.**

Now imagine you sell 50 products and need 3 ad variations each. That's 150 videos. At even $1,000 per video, you're looking at $150,000 and months of production time.

Even if you go the DIY route — shooting on your phone, editing yourself — you're still spending hours per video and the quality often isn't competitive.

This is exactly why AI video ads have exploded in popularity. They collapse the entire production process into minutes.

## 5 Types of AI Video Ads Every E-Commerce Brand Needs

### 1. Product Showcase Ads (15 seconds)
Quick, punchy videos that highlight your product's best features. Perfect for cold audiences who need a fast introduction. Use these for Instagram Stories and TikTok feed.

### 2. Explainer/Demo Ads (30-60 seconds)
Longer videos where an avatar walks through how the product works, its key benefits, and why it's different. These are your workhorses for Facebook and Instagram feed ads. The 60-second format is especially powerful — long enough to educate, short enough to hold attention.

### 3. Testimonial-Style Ads
AI avatars delivering "review-style" content: "I've been using this for 3 weeks and here's what happened..." These mimic UGC and build social proof without needing actual customers on camera.

### 4. Comparison Ads
"I tried 5 different [products] and this one won." Comparison content performs exceptionally well because it pre-answers the "why this one?" question that every buyer has.

### 5. Seasonal/Flash Sale Ads
Urgency-driven ads for sales events, festivals, and limited-time offers. AI lets you spin these up same-day — no need to plan a shoot weeks in advance for your Diwali sale or Black Friday campaign.

> 💡 **The winning combination:** Use 15-second product showcase ads for prospecting, then retarget viewers with 60-second explainer ads. This two-step approach consistently outperforms single-ad strategies. With Zockto, you can create both formats from the same product upload.

## Category-Specific Playbooks

Different product categories need different approaches. Here's what works best:

### Health & Supplements
- **Focus on:** Benefits, ingredients, results timelines
- **Best format:** 45-60 second explainer with avatar describing personal experience
- **Hook examples:** "I wish I'd started this sooner" / "3 signs you need [supplement]"
- **Compliance tip:** Avoid medical claims. Focus on wellness and lifestyle benefits.

### Skincare & Beauty
- **Focus on:** Before/after potential, texture, routine integration
- **Best format:** 30-second "Get Ready With Me" style
- **Hook examples:** "My dermatologist asked what I changed" / "This replaced 3 products in my routine"

### Jewelry & Accessories
- **Focus on:** Styling, gifting occasions, craftsmanship
- **Best format:** 15-second showcase + 30-second styling guide
- **Hook examples:** "The necklace everyone asks about" / "Under ₹2,000 and looks like luxury"

### Home Decor
- **Focus on:** Room transformation, ambiance, design versatility
- **Best format:** 30-second room reveal with avatar narration
- **Hook examples:** "This ₹500 piece changed my whole living room" / "Interior designers hate this trick"

### Consumer Tech
- **Focus on:** Features, specs comparison, use cases
- **Best format:** 60-second detailed walkthrough
- **Hook examples:** "I replaced my [expensive product] with this" / "The feature nobody talks about"

## Measuring Success: KPIs That Matter

Don't just create video ads and hope for the best. Track these metrics:

- **Hook Rate (3-second views / impressions):** Aim for 30%+. Below 20% means your hook needs work.
- **ThruPlay Rate (completed views / impressions):** 15%+ for 15-second ads, 8%+ for 60-second ads.
- **Click-Through Rate (CTR):** 1.5%+ for cold audiences, 3%+ for retargeting.
- **Cost Per Click (CPC):** Video ads should achieve 20-40% lower CPC than image ads.
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** The only metric that truly matters. Compare video vs. image ROAS head-to-head.
