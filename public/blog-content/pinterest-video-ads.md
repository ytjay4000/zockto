## Why Pinterest Is a Goldmine for Product Brands

Before we dive into video specifics, let's talk about why Pinterest deserves a spot in your ad mix:

- **High purchase intent:** 85% of weekly Pinterest users have bought something based on pins they've seen. These aren't casual scrollers — they're active shoppers.
- **Long content lifespan:** A pin's half-life is 3.5 months, compared to a tweet (18 minutes) or Instagram post (48 hours). Your ad content keeps working long after the campaign ends.
- **Unbranded discovery:** 97% of searches are unbranded. A user searching "minimalist gold necklace" is equally likely to discover your brand as any competitor's.
- **Premium demographics:** Pinterest users tend to have higher household income and are in "planning mode" — actively looking for products to buy.
- **Lower competition:** While every brand fights for attention on Meta and Google, Pinterest remains relatively underutilized, meaning lower CPMs and higher ROAS.

For product categories like home decor, jewelry, skincare, fashion, and health supplements, Pinterest isn't just "another channel" — it might be your best channel.

## The Power of Video on Pinterest

Pinterest has invested heavily in video over the past two years, and the results for advertisers have been dramatic:

- **2x higher engagement** than static pins

Video works on Pinterest because the platform is inherently visual. When someone is scrolling through a board of "living room ideas" and your home decor product appears as a beautiful video instead of a static image, it stops the scroll. It tells a story. It shows the product in context.

## Pinterest Video Ad Formats

### Standard Video Pins

These appear in the regular Pinterest feed alongside organic content. They autoplay in the feed and expand when tapped. Best aspect ratio: 2:3 (vertical) or 1:1 (square). This is your bread-and-butter format for product discovery.

### Max-Width Video Pins

These span the full width of the mobile feed — two columns instead of one. Much more attention-grabbing and perfect for brand awareness campaigns. Uses 16:9 landscape format. Higher cost but dramatically higher visibility.

### Idea Pins with Video

Multi-page story-style pins that can include video, images, and text. Great for tutorials, how-to content, and product demonstrations. Think of them as Pinterest's version of Instagram Stories, but with evergreen staying power.

### Shopping Video Ads

Video pins with tagged products that link directly to your product page. Users can see price, availability, and buy directly. This is the holy grail for e-commerce — combining the engagement of video with the convenience of shoppable tags.

## Video Specs & Technical Requirements

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Format | MP4, MOV |
| Resolution | Minimum 720p (1080p recommended) |
| Aspect Ratio | 2:3 (standard), 1:1 (square), 16:9 (max-width) |
| Duration | 4s to 15min (15-30s recommended for ads) |
| File Size | Under 2GB |
| Audio | Optional (autoplay is muted) |

> 💡 **Pinterest Pro Tip:** Always create your Pinterest video ads in 2:3 vertical format first — this takes up the most feed real estate on mobile. Then create a 1:1 version for placement variety. Vertical videos on Pinterest get 60% more engagement than landscape.

## Creative Best Practices for Pinterest Video Ads

Pinterest video ads that convert share these characteristics:

### Lead with the Product

Pinterest users are product-curious. Show your product within the first 2 seconds. Not your logo, not a lifestyle montage — the actual product. Beautiful, well-lit, and aspirational.

### Make It Aesthetically Pinterest-Native

Pinterest has a distinct visual culture — clean, aspirational, well-styled. Your video should feel like it belongs in someone's board, not like a TV commercial. Think warm lighting, lifestyle context, and minimal text overlays.

### Design for Sound-Off

Like most social platforms, Pinterest videos autoplay muted. Your video needs to work perfectly without sound. Use visual storytelling, elegant text overlays, and product close-ups that speak for themselves.

### Show the Product in Context

A necklace on a white background is fine. A necklace being worn at a dinner party, catching the light, paired with an outfit? That's a Pinterest save. Context helps buyers envision the product in their own life.

### Include a Text Overlay CTA

End with a clear text overlay: "Shop Now," "Tap to Explore," or "Save for Later." Pinterest users are primed to act — give them the nudge.

### Keep It Short

15-30 seconds is the sweet spot for **Pinterest ad creative**. You can go longer for tutorial-style content, but for product ads, shorter is almost always better.

## Targeting Strategies That Work

Pinterest's targeting options are uniquely powerful because of the platform's search-driven nature:

### Keyword Targeting

This is Pinterest's superpower. Target specific search terms like "minimalist bedroom ideas," "best vitamin C serum," or "gold hoop earrings." You're reaching people at the exact moment they're searching for what you sell.

### Interest Targeting

Reach users based on their board topics and engagement patterns. Pinterest knows what people are interested in based on years of saving and searching behavior — this data is remarkably accurate.

### Audience Targeting

- **Customer lists:** Upload your email list and target existing customers with new product videos
- **Website visitors:** Retarget people who browsed your site with product-specific video ads
- **Actalike audiences:** Pinterest's version of lookalikes — find users similar to your best customers
- **Engagement audiences:** Target people who've saved or clicked your pins before

### Shopping Targeting

If you've connected your product catalog, Pinterest can automatically show your video ads to users searching for products in your category. This combines the power of video with shopping intent.

Once your campaigns are running, here's how to optimize and scale:
