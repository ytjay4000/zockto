## Feature Comparison

### Video Length — Advantage: Zockto

This is the most significant feature gap between the two platforms. **Zockto supports 60-second videos; Creatify caps at approximately 30 seconds.**

Why 60 seconds matters:
- **YouTube ads:** The most effective YouTube ad format is 30-60 seconds. With Creatify's 30-second cap, you're hitting the absolute minimum. Zockto gives you room to breathe.
- **Complex products:** Supplements, tech gadgets, skincare routines — anything that needs explanation beyond a quick hook benefits from 45-60 seconds.
- **Story-driven ads:** The problem → solution → proof → CTA framework takes 35-50 seconds to execute well. Hard to fit in 30 seconds without rushing.
- **Facebook Feed:** Longer videos in Feed consistently show higher conversion rates for products over ₹1,000.

If your entire strategy is 15-second TikTok clips and Instagram Reels, the 30-second cap is fine. But most serious e-commerce advertisers need longer formats as part of their creative mix.

### Avatar Library — Advantage: Zockto

Zockto offers 150+ avatars compared to Creatify's 70+. More importantly, Zockto includes **avatar cloning** — you can create a custom AI avatar based on your own likeness or your brand ambassador's. Creatify doesn't offer this feature.

Avatar cloning is particularly valuable for brands that want consistency. Create one avatar that represents your brand and use it across all your ads. It's also powerful for personal brands and influencer-driven D2C companies.

### Voice — Advantage: Zockto

Zockto offers 200+ voice options with voice cloning capability. Creatify offers voice options but doesn't support voice cloning.

Voice cloning lets you record a sample of your voice (or a brand voice) and use it across all your ads. This creates audio brand consistency that's hard to achieve with generic voice libraries.

### Language Support — Advantage: Zockto

Zockto supports 48 languages; Creatify supports 29. For brands selling in India (where you might want ads in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and Marathi simultaneously) or targeting international markets, the broader language support is a practical advantage.

### URL-to-Video — Close Call

Both tools offer URL-to-video functionality. Creatify was one of the first tools to really nail this feature, and it works well — paste a product URL, and the AI analyzes the page to generate a script and video.

Zockto's URL-to-video is also solid and feeds into its simplified 5-click workflow. Both tools handle this well; neither has a dramatic advantage here.

### AI Product Photography — Advantage: Zockto

Zockto's Product Ad Shot feature generates professional product photography using AI. Place your product in studio-quality lifestyle scenes without a photoshoot. Creatify doesn't offer this.

This is a genuinely useful bonus for e-commerce brands that need both video ads and product images. Having both in one tool saves you from paying for a separate product photography service.

### Batch Creation — Advantage: Creatify

Creatify has a strong batch creation feature that lets you generate multiple ad variations simultaneously. This is useful for performance marketers who need to test many creatives quickly.

Zockto's workflow is fast for individual videos, and you can create variations by swapping avatars and templates quickly, but Creatify's dedicated batch feature is more streamlined for high-volume variation generation.

## Workflow and UX

**Zockto's workflow** is built around the "5 clicks to video" concept. The entire process is streamlined to minimize decisions: upload product → choose avatar → pick template → generate → download. It's opinionated by design — fewer options means faster output.

**Creatify's workflow** is slightly more involved but still intuitive. The URL-to-video flow is well-designed, and the interface gives you a bit more control over scripting and editing. For users who want to tweak their scripts before generating, Creatify provides better pre-generation editing.

The UX philosophies are different:
- **Zockto:** "Let's get you a video as fast as possible." — Best for volume and speed.
- **Creatify:** "Let's give you some control while keeping it simple." — Best for marketers who want to refine before generating.

Both approaches are valid. If you're producing 20+ ads per week and want maximum speed, Zockto's minimalism is an advantage. If you prefer to review and adjust scripts before committing credits, Creatify gives you that option.
