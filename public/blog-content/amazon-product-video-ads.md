## Types of Amazon Product Videos

Amazon supports video in several places. Understanding each type helps you prioritize:

### 1. Product Listing Videos

Videos that appear in your product image carousel on the listing page. Any brand-registered seller can add these. They're free to upload and are one of the highest-impact additions you can make to any listing.

### 2. A+ Content Videos

Videos embedded in your A+ (Enhanced Brand Content) section below the bullet points. These are part of the rich content that tells your brand story. Available to brand-registered sellers.

### 3. Sponsored Brand Video Ads

Paid video ads that appear in search results when shoppers search for relevant keywords. These auto-play as users scroll, giving you a massive visibility advantage over static Sponsored Products ads.

### 4. Amazon Posts Videos

Social-media-style content in Amazon's Posts feed. Think of it as Instagram, but on Amazon. Videos here help build brand awareness and drive organic traffic to your listings.

### 5. Amazon Live

Live-streaming on Amazon's platform. While not technically a "video ad," it's a powerful way to demonstrate products in real-time. Best for brands with high engagement and a storytelling angle.

## Product Listing Videos: The Basics

If you're going to add video to one place on Amazon, start here. Product listing videos appear in the image carousel and play when a customer clicks on them. They're the most-viewed video format on Amazon.

### Technical Requirements

- **Format:** MP4 or MOV
- **Resolution:** Minimum 720p, recommended 1080p
- **Length:** 6 seconds to 5 minutes (aim for 30-90 seconds)
- **File size:** Maximum 500MB
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or 1:1 (16:9 is preferred for desktop, 1:1 for mobile)

### Content Guidelines

Amazon is strict about what you can and can't include:

- ❌ No pricing information
- ❌ No promotional claims ("50% off!" "Best deal!")
- ❌ No external website URLs
- ❌ No customer reviews or testimonials (for listing videos specifically)
- ✅ Product features and benefits
- ✅ Product in use / demonstration
- ✅ Size and dimensions visualization
- ✅ How-to and tutorial content

### What Makes a Great Listing Video

The best Amazon listing videos follow a simple formula:

1. **Open with the product** — no logos, no lengthy intros. Show the product immediately
2. **Demonstrate key features** — show, don't tell. If it's waterproof, pour water on it
3. **Address the top concern** — what's the #1 question in your reviews? Answer it visually
4. **Show scale and context** — in someone's hands, on a table, in a room. Give buyers a sense of size
5. **End with the product name** — simple text overlay with your brand and product name

> "The #1 reason customers leave an Amazon listing without buying is uncertainty. Video eliminates uncertainty. It shows the product in real context, at real scale, doing real things. That's why it converts."

## Sponsored Brand Video Ads Deep Dive

Sponsored Brand Video (SBV) ads are arguably the most powerful ad format on Amazon. Here's why — and how to use them effectively.

### How They Work

SBV ads appear in search results when a shopper searches for a relevant keyword. Your video auto-plays (muted) as the shopper scrolls past. If it catches their eye and they click, they go directly to your product listing.

The auto-play is the key. Unlike other Amazon ad formats that rely on static images and headlines, SBV ads have motion and movement. In a sea of static search results, a playing video is an attention magnet.

### Campaign Setup

1. Go to Amazon Ads → Sponsored Brands → Create Campaign
2. Select "Video" as your ad format
3. Choose the product to promote
4. Upload your video (6-45 seconds, 16:9 or 1:1, 1080p recommended)
5. Add keywords (exact, phrase, or broad match)
6. Set your bid and daily budget
7. Launch

### Keyword Strategy for SBV

Your keyword strategy for video ads should differ from regular Sponsored Products:

- **Branded keywords:** Defend your brand terms with video (huge impact on branded search conversion)
- **Competitor keywords:** Show your video when people search for competitors — the visual advantage is massive
- **Category keywords:** Broad category terms work well because the video creative does the qualifying
- **Problem keywords:** Target search terms that describe the problem your product solves

> 🔥 **SBV Pro Tip:** Run Sponsored Brand Video ads on your competitor's brand keywords. When someone searches for "[Competitor] face serum," your video ad showing a real person demonstrating YOUR serum is incredibly effective. The video format gives you an unfair advantage over their static listings.

## Best Practices for Amazon Video Ads

### 1. Front-Load the Value

Amazon videos auto-play muted in most placements. Your first 2-3 seconds need to be visually compelling enough to make someone tap and unmute. Show the product doing something interesting immediately — don't waste time with brand logos or text-heavy intros.

### 2. Design for Muted Viewing

While audio matters, your video should be understandable without it. Use text overlays for key points, captions for spoken content, and clear visual demonstrations. Think of audio as a bonus, not a requirement.

### 3. Address the Top Review Concerns

Go to your (or your competitor's) reviews. What are the top 3 questions or concerns? Your video should answer them visually. "Is it big enough?" Show scale. "Does it actually work?" Show a demo. "Is it easy to set up?" Show the process.

### 4. Keep It Focused

One video, one message. Don't try to cover every feature and benefit in a single video. For SBV ads, focus on one compelling hook. For listing videos, focus on the top 3 features.

### 5. Test Multiple Creatives

For SBV campaigns, run 3-5 different video creatives simultaneously. Amazon's reporting will show you which gets the best click-through and conversion rates. Kill the losers, create new variations of the winners.

### 6. Optimize for Mobile

Over 70% of Amazon shopping happens on mobile. Your text overlays need to be large enough to read on small screens. 1:1 aspect ratio often performs better than 16:9 on mobile because it takes up more screen real estate.

### 7. Include a Clear CTA

Even though Amazon handles the "Buy Now" button, your video should end with a clear call to action. "See it in action — scroll through the images" or "Check the full feature list below." Guide the viewer's next step.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Making the Video Too Long

For SBV ads, 15-30 seconds is the sweet spot. For listing videos, 45-90 seconds. Going longer than that and you lose most viewers. Every second should deliver value — if a scene doesn't add anything, cut it.

### ❌ Showing the Product Too Late

Shoppers are on Amazon to see products. If your video spends the first 10 seconds on a mood montage before showing the product, you've lost them. Product first, story second.

### ❌ Ignoring Amazon's Content Guidelines

Amazon will reject videos that include pricing, "best seller" claims, time-sensitive promotions, or external URLs. Review the guidelines before production to avoid wasting time on non-compliant content.

### ❌ Using Only One Video Per Listing

Amazon lets you upload multiple videos to a listing. Use this. A demo video, a how-to video, and a lifestyle video each serve different buyer needs. More videos = more engagement = more conversions.

### ❌ Not Localizing for International Markets

If you sell on Amazon.de, Amazon.co.jp, or Amazon.in, your English video isn't going to cut it. Localize your videos for each market. With AI tools, this is trivial — regenerate the same video in German, Japanese, or Hindi in minutes.

### ❌ Treating Video as a One-Time Task

Video isn't "set and forget." Update your videos when you improve your product, get new feature ideas from reviews, or want to test new angles. The brands that continuously iterate on their Amazon video content consistently outperform those that upload once and move on.

> "The biggest mistake Amazon sellers make with video isn't creating bad videos — it's not creating any at all. Even a simple AI-generated product video outperforms having no video on your listing."

## Measuring Video Ad Performance

### For Sponsored Brand Video Ads

| Metric | What It Means | Good Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Video Completion Rate | % who watched the full video | 15-30% |
| Click-Through Rate (CTR) | % who clicked to your listing | 0.4-1.5% |
| ACoS | Ad spend as % of attributed sales | 15-30% |
| ROAS | Revenue per ad dollar | 3-7x |
| New-to-Brand % | % of customers who are new | 60-80% |

### For Listing Videos

Amazon doesn't give you direct metrics on listing video views (frustrating, right?). But you can measure impact indirectly:

- **Conversion rate change:** Track your listing's conversion rate before and after adding video
- **Session duration:** Available in Brand Analytics — does it increase after adding video?
- **Return rate:** Should decrease as video gives buyers better expectations
- **Sales velocity:** The most important metric — are you selling more?

### The Testing Framework

For SBV campaigns, follow this testing rhythm:

1. **Week 1-2:** Launch 3-5 video creatives on your top keywords
2. **Week 3:** Analyze CTR and ACoS per creative. Pause bottom performers
3. **Week 4:** Create 2-3 new variations inspired by the winners
4. **Monthly:** Refresh your best-performing creatives with updated versions
