## What to Look for in an AI UGC Tool

Before we dive into individual tools, let's talk about what actually matters when choosing an **AI UGC video generator**:

- **Video length:** Can it produce videos beyond 15-30 seconds? Longer ads (45-60s) convert better on YouTube and Facebook.
- **Avatar quality:** Do the avatars look natural? Can you clone your own?
- **Voice quality:** Natural-sounding voices with emotion, not robotic TTS.
- **Language support:** Essential if you sell globally or in multilingual markets like India.
- **Ease of use:** How many clicks from product image to finished video?
- **Price per video:** The real metric — not monthly cost, but cost per usable output.
- **Output resolution:** 1080p is table stakes. Anything less is a dealbreaker.

With that framework in mind, let's look at each tool.

3. **Quality review:** Evaluated avatar realism, lip-sync accuracy, voice naturalness, and visual polish.
4. **Cost per video:** Calculated the actual cost per usable video based on each plan's credit system.
5. **Feature depth:** Tested advanced features like cloning, multi-language support, and batch generation.
